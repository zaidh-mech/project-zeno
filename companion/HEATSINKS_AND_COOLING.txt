AURA R1 — HEATSINKS AND COOLING

No add-on heatsink is specified in the current BOM or CAD. A particular sink
size cannot be qualified until the received modules and temperatures are known.

TP4056 CHARGER — first priority during charging.
At 5 V input, 3 V cell voltage and 0.4 A, calculated chip dissipation is about
0.8 W. Its exposed underside pad must transfer heat into PCB copper. Keep the
board surfaces clear; a small adhesive sink on top is not a validated solution.
Reduce charge current if needed, then recheck charge termination and cell
temperature. Do not use thermal foldback as the normal operating condition.

MT3608 BOOST CONVERTER — first priority during operation.
At 5 V / 0.5 A, estimated whole-module loss is 0.28–0.63 W if efficiency is
90–80%; actual efficiency is unmeasured. Check the IC, diode AND inductor.
Use PCB heat spreading and ventilation first. If temperatures are excessive,
reduce the load or redesign with a measured, electrically isolated metal
spreader and a heat path out of the case. Added metal requires a CAD fit check.

MAX98357A AMPLIFIER — no external heatsink expected initially at verified
9 dB gain and the 45% PCM cap. Confirm the breakout's gain strap; GAIN_SLOT
unconnected selects 9 dB. Higher gain can exceed the assumed power budget.
Use its PCB ground plane for cooling and test at the permitted audio load.

ESP32-C3 — no external heatsink selected. Check MCU and onboard regulator
temperatures during sustained Wi-Fi. Keep future metal away from the antenna.
LCD, touch sensor and speaker — no additional sink selected.
18650 CELL — do not attach a hot module or heatsink to it; preserve separation.

ENCLOSURE
Three low front slots (4 x 1.4 mm) and four high rear outlets (7.6 x 1.4 mm)
provide a passive air route separate from the sealed speaker grille. Keep
wires and foam clear of these openings. Cooling performance is unvalidated.
Test the closed PETG case at intended ambient temperature during a full charge
cycle and sustained Wi-Fi/audio, including near-depleted battery operation.
Compare results with the authentic cell, component and filament limits.

Heat-set inserts are different from heatsinks: this design uses 18 brass
M3 x 4 x 5 inserts. Their positions are in research/insert_locations.csv.
